# After Action Report

## Executive Summary

TechNova conducted a simulated ransomware tabletop exercise to evaluate cross-functional incident response, business continuity, recovery, notification, and executive decision-making. The exercise achieved its primary objective but identified five governance and preparedness gaps requiring tracked remediation.

## Objective Ratings

| Objective | Rating | Observation |
|---|---|---|
| Detection and escalation | Met | Participants identified the incident and escalation path quickly. |
| Incident command | Partially Met | An incident commander was assigned, but backup contacts were outdated. |
| Containment and evidence | Met | Isolation and evidence-preservation actions were appropriately sequenced. |
| Business continuity | Partially Met | Recovery priorities were known, but the support-platform workaround was incomplete. |
| Legal and notification | Partially Met | Legal joined early, but the jurisdiction matrix lacked required detail. |
| Recovery | Partially Met | Backups were available, but a Tier 1 platform had not been included in recent restoration testing. |
| Executive decisions | Partially Met | Ransom-payment authority existed informally but lacked documented criteria. |

## Strengths

- Fast escalation from service desk to Security.
- Clear incident commander selection.
- Appropriate isolation and evidence-preservation discussion.
- Early involvement of Legal, Privacy, and Communications.
- Availability of offline backups and a clean recovery environment.

## Areas for Improvement

1. Update the incident-response contact roster and test the call tree quarterly.
2. Document ransomware payment authority, legal review, sanctions checks, and risk-acceptance criteria.
3. Include every Tier 1 service in restoration testing.
4. Build a jurisdiction-specific breach-notification matrix.
5. Approve internal, customer, regulator, and media communication templates.

## Conclusion

TechNova demonstrated a functional response capability but requires stronger documentation and recurring validation in several governance areas. Corrective actions have been assigned through the improvement plan and should be validated in a follow-up exercise within six months.
