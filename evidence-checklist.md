# Ransomware Readiness Evidence Checklist

## Governance

- [ ] Approved incident-response plan
- [ ] Incident-response roles and RACI matrix
- [ ] Current primary and alternate contact roster
- [ ] Incident severity and declaration criteria
- [ ] Executive risk-acceptance authority
- [ ] Ransomware payment decision framework

## Technical Response

- [ ] Endpoint detection alerts
- [ ] Centralized authentication and system logs
- [ ] Privileged access and MFA records
- [ ] Network isolation procedure
- [ ] Evidence preservation and chain-of-custody form
- [ ] Compromised credential reset procedure

## Business Continuity and Recovery

- [ ] Business impact analysis
- [ ] Critical-service inventory and dependencies
- [ ] Documented RTO and RPO targets
- [ ] Offline or immutable backup evidence
- [ ] Recent restoration-test results
- [ ] Clean recovery environment procedure
- [ ] Service validation and incident closure checklist

## Legal and Communications

- [ ] Cyber-insurance policy and contact details
- [ ] External counsel and law-enforcement contacts
- [ ] Regulatory and contractual notification matrix
- [ ] Customer, employee, regulator, and media templates
- [ ] Communication approval workflow

## Corrective Action

- [ ] Completed attendance record
- [ ] Exercise decision log
- [ ] Objective evaluation worksheets
- [ ] After-action report
- [ ] Improvement plan with owners and due dates
- [ ] Management-approved residual risk decisions
