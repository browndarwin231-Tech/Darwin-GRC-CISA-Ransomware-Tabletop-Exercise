# TechNova Ransomware Tabletop Exercise Plan

## Exercise Details

- **Exercise type:** Discussion-based tabletop exercise
- **Organization:** TechNova fictional SaaS company
- **Duration:** 120 minutes
- **Threat:** Ransomware with suspected data exfiltration
- **Facilitator:** GRC Analyst
- **Classification:** Educational portfolio simulation

## Scope

The exercise covers TechNova's identity environment, Windows server infrastructure, customer-support platform, cloud backups, incident communications, regulatory obligations, and executive decision-making. Destructive technical testing is out of scope.

## Objectives

1. Validate detection, triage, escalation, and incident declaration.
2. Confirm the roles of the incident commander and participating teams.
3. Evaluate containment choices and evidence preservation.
4. Test recovery priorities, backup validation, and business continuity.
5. Review legal, privacy, insurance, law-enforcement, and notification decisions.
6. Record gaps and create accountable corrective actions.

## Participants and Responsibilities

| Role | Exercise responsibility |
|---|---|
| Executive Sponsor | Business priorities and risk decisions |
| Incident Commander | Coordinates response and approves operational actions |
| Security Lead | Investigation, scoping, containment, and evidence |
| IT Operations Lead | System isolation, backup validation, and restoration |
| Legal and Privacy | Privilege, notification, contractual, and regulatory analysis |
| Communications Lead | Internal, customer, media, and regulator messaging |
| Business Continuity Lead | Critical-service priorities and manual workarounds |
| GRC Facilitator | Presents injects, records decisions, and evaluates objectives |

## Assumptions and Rules

- Participants respond using current documented plans and available resources.
- No real systems are changed during the exercise.
- The scenario advances regardless of participant decisions.
- Participants may identify missing information as a gap.
- Discussion focuses on policy, coordination, authority, evidence, and business impact.

## Schedule

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0-10 minutes | Welcome, scope, rules, and objectives |
| 10-25 minutes | Initial detection and triage |
| 25-50 minutes | Containment and incident command |
| 50-75 minutes | Exfiltration, legal, and communication decisions |
| 75-100 minutes | Recovery and business continuity |
| 100-115 minutes | Executive decisions and lessons learned |
| 115-120 minutes | Hotwash and next steps |

## Evaluation Method

Each objective is rated **Met**, **Partially Met**, or **Not Met** based on decisions, role clarity, documented procedures, evidence availability, and the ability to define a practical next action.
