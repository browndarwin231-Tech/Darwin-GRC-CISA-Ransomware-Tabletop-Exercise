# Facilitator Guide

## Before the Exercise

- Confirm participants and alternates.
- Distribute the participant guide without the inject details.
- Review the incident-response, continuity, recovery, and notification plans.
- Assign a note taker and timekeeper.
- Prepare an attendance record and decision log.

## Opening Script

This is a no-fault, discussion-based exercise. The scenario is fictional and no production systems will be changed. Participants should describe what they would do using current plans, tools, authorities, and communication paths. Missing information should be recorded as an improvement opportunity.

## Facilitation Sequence

### Inject 1 Initial Detection

Employees report ransom notes and inaccessible shared files.

**Ask:** Who validates the event? What criteria trigger incident declaration? Who must be notified first?

### Inject 2 Privileged Account Compromise

The security team identifies suspicious use of a dormant administrator account.

**Ask:** What containment actions are authorized? How are credentials revoked without disrupting recovery? How is evidence preserved?

### Inject 3 Business Disruption

The customer-support platform becomes unavailable and call volume increases.

**Ask:** Who sets recovery priorities? What manual workaround exists? When is leadership briefed?

### Inject 4 Suspected Exfiltration

Threat actors provide a sample of customer data and threaten publication.

**Ask:** Who determines whether a breach occurred? Which contracts or jurisdictions may require notification? Who contacts cyber insurance and law enforcement?

### Inject 5 Ransom Demand

The attacker demands payment within 24 hours.

**Ask:** Who has payment authority? What legal and sanctions checks are required? Which alternatives must be evaluated?

### Inject 6 Recovery Complication

Backups appear intact, but the customer-support restoration procedure has not been tested recently.

**Ask:** How is a clean recovery environment established? What evidence proves backup integrity? Who accepts the recovery risk?

## Hotwash Questions

- What worked well?
- Where was ownership unclear?
- Which decisions lacked documented criteria?
- What evidence or contact information was missing?
- Which actions should be completed within 30, 60, and 90 days?

## Evaluation Standard

Record decisions and supporting procedures. Rate each objective based on demonstrated readiness rather than intended future capability.
