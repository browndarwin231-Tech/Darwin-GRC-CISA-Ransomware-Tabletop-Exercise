# Participant Guide

## Purpose

This exercise tests TechNova's ability to coordinate a ransomware response across technical, legal, business, and executive functions.

## Participant Expectations

- Respond according to current procedures and authority.
- State assumptions when information is unavailable.
- Identify the person or team responsible for each decision.
- Describe required evidence, approvals, and communications.
- Treat gaps as improvement opportunities rather than individual failures.

## Discussion Areas

- Incident detection and declaration
- Command structure and escalation
- Containment and evidence preservation
- Business impact and continuity
- Backup validation and system restoration
- Legal, privacy, insurance, and notification obligations
- Customer, employee, regulator, and media communications
- Ransom decision-making and executive risk acceptance

## Success Criteria

The exercise succeeds when participants can explain who acts, what they do, when escalation occurs, which evidence supports the decision, and how identified gaps will be corrected.
